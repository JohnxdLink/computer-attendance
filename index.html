About / Problem

Attendance still runs on paper, in a school built for the digital age.

"Why does this matter?"

Manual attendance takes time is prone to errors and makes it difficult to monitor student presence. This systen helps solve these problems and improves attendance management for Grade 12 students at Britech College
✕

Manual attendance

  • Takes up class time to call names or pass a sheet
  • Prone to human error and mismarked entries
  • Makes it difficult to monitor student presence in real time
  • Records are harder to search, total, and report on
VS
✓

Computerized system

  • Logs a student's presence in seconds at check-in
  • Reduces mismarked or missed entries
  • Gives staff a live, accurate view of who's present
  • Turns attendance into data that's easy to review

Manual attendance-taking takes time, is prone to errors, and makes it difficult to monitor student presence. This study looks at whether a computerized system helps solve those problems and how much it improves accuracy and efficiency for Grade 12 Alcaraz at Britech College.
